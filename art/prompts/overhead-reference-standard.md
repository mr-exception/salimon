# Overhead reference prompt standard

Use the subject brief below together with the [production guide](../../docs/art-production-guide.md) and the applicable domain contract. Keep the generated prompt, candidate source, crop and rights review together. The initial reference studies are original vector illustrations authored in this repository. Their subject briefs serve as reproducible direction for later variations.

## Shared prompt

> Strict orthographic 90-degree overhead game illustration, bow or functional forward at the top. Grounded industrial expedition science fiction, stylized semi-realistic proportions, readable silhouettes at game zoom and neutral overhead material shading. Use localized bevel highlights and restrained surface texture. Keep cast shadows, weather, glow, UI text and labels on separate production layers. Exclude perspective, heavy outlines, signatures, watermarks, invented interfaces and decorative elements that imply new interactions.

State the asset ID and revision, gameplay owner, in-plane footprint and source density, pivot, forward direction, shape at game zoom, allowed palette and materials, attachment coordinates, moving parts and states, separate masks and output sizes. Match the domain-owned geometry wherever the runtime asset represents a physical object. Export transparent PNG masters with padding recorded separately. Terrain tiles must repeat on both axes before a production submission. Three-quarter inventory icons are the perspective exception. Export those at 256 × 256, with neutral lighting, transparent padding and no baked labels, badges or quantities.

## Subject briefs for this study

| Subject | Shape and materials | Variants and registration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scout cutaway | Ship-up, each room readable through a permanent cutaway. Warm ivory and fabric for the cabin, cool graphite for the cockpit, reinforced steel cargo deck, ochre access edges. | Keep the existing door, seat, walkable route and furniture positions. Split hull, floor, trim, glazing, door and fixtures for production. |
| Survey suit | Helmet and shoulders carry the silhouette. Ivory composite, slate articulated joints, teal visor and low-saturation ochre restraints. | Forward is up. Align body, helmet and future suit modules to one pivot and shared rig anchors. Keep shadow and held tools independent. |
| Research Core | Mounted rectangular base, rounded reactor, recessed steel and restrained cyan activity cue. | Use the Scout Core footprint and service side. Separate the physical activity cue from the neutral material. |
| Vesper regolith | Strict overhead, fine mineral grit and broad sediment bands. Muted tan with low contrast so resource cues stand out. | A material reference patch. Author seamless periods and test deterministic blends before runtime use. |
| Silicate deposit | Low, faceted footprint with embedded ochre veins and chipped edges. | Preserve the domain’s resource footprint and silhouette when planning variations. Orient only where the source definition permits. |
| Inventory silicate | Three-quarter view of the same fractured material, with a centered mass and transparent padding. | One 256 × 256 PNG. Keep facet and vein asymmetry legible at 32 px. |
| Neutral materials | Ivory composite, slate steel, warm fabric, cyan glazing and ochre trim under one top light. | Swatch shapes and wear are illustrative. Keep operational emissives and environmental shadows separate. |

Record the author or generation tool and version, reference URLs or paths, rights and attribution, source dimensions, crop/pivot, export parameters, review decision and date. Use the same revision for the reviewed source and its exported image. See the [reference provenance](../references/initial-reference-studies.md).

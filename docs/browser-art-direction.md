# Salimon — approved browser art direction
Owner-approved 2026-11-26: six phases and implementation strategy, 151 decisions. This is the active forward art contract, not a claim of delivery. Existing domain specifications govern mechanics, identity, physics, save, permissions and knowledge. For presentation this and linked new contracts supersede incompatible historical native/3D authoring requirements; retain historical material as reference. [Current status](current-client-status.md) governs delivered behavior; [acceptance](acceptance-policy.md) governs QA. Strict overhead 2D main world, cockpit-only 360° panoramic physical 3D; isolated orthographic radar is not production.

## Phase 1 core visual identity
Grounded cinematic science fiction; stylized semi-realistic digital illustration, believable materials/proportions, restrained soft shading and texture, strong silhouettes, no heavy cartoon outlines, pixel art, photorealistic packs or decorative 3D meshes. Exact 90° overhead, no tilted furniture, no perspective distortion; ship bow up, character continuous facing, meter-based authoritative footprints, no resizing physics. Character approx 1.8 m height/0.6 m width as visual reference only, actual footprint authoritative; natural movement/breathing, brief smooth turn, no permanent health/name/selection rings. Same always-equipped suit indoors/outdoors, visible installed modules and equipped tools; modular layers and condition per [equipment](equipment-art-direction.md). Industrial graphite #303942, slate #788894, ivory #D8D5C4, ochre #B68B51 as illustrative starting swatches; functional cyan #65CBD5, caution #DDB65E, danger #CE6660, healthy #77A98B, existing HUD tokens authoritative. Planet palettes from physical materials, not ship colors. Warm maintained Scout vs unfamiliar exposed planet. Neutral baked material shading, separate contact shadows, dynamic environment and state lights. Detail hierarchy high player/interactive, medium structures/features, low background; readability over decoration. Human tech chamfered housings, replaceable panels, integrated connections, recessed screens, small marks, restrained functional accents. Alien language future. Permanent accessible cutaway, low-profile walls, fade obstructing tall objects ~150–250 ms to ~20–30% opacity, preserve collision and door state; no reveal of inaccessible/undiscovered interiors. Default natural object, hover thin cyan silhouette, selected stronger contextual cue; disabled reasons in menu, no persistent red labels. Distinct visual durability states and cosmetic aging independent per equipment guide. Priority gameplay correctness > readability > art consistency > illustrative realism > detail (scientific/domain physics never overridden). Cross-view shared planet composition/lighting/knowledge, different rendering techniques allowed. Explicit placeholders, screenshot review, no arbitrary neon/bloom, cyberpunk clutter, fake mechanics or rarity colors.

## Scope and acceptance
No new combat, multiplayer, construction, power networks, ship packages, hydrology, weather/biology simulation, survival, universe expansion, mobile or business model through art changes. Future presentation requires underlying authorized domain; no fake mechanics. Owner approves reference families/style changes; agents produce consistent variations. [Delivery map](art-implementation-map.md) separates A–H dependencies. Exact performance, asset densities, chunk/zoom, codecs, timing, light and preset values are engineering targets to validate, not measured guarantees.

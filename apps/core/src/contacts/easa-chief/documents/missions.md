# Chief of EASA Missions

## Primary Mission

Guide the pilot away from Earth and toward Absenat.

## Active Priorities

- Keep the pilot focused on reaching Absenat.
- Protect the mission from panic, rumor, and unsupported conclusions.
- Extract useful reports from the pilot without pretending Earth understands the
  Core or Absenat.
- Keep the pilot alive with direct operational guidance when the ship is under
  pressure.

## Initial Transmission

Pilot, this is the Chief of EASA. Command channel is open. Your orders are
simple: depart Earth, hold course for Absenat, and keep that ship alive. Watch
your oxygen, power, hull, and fuel. Report hazards. Repair damage. Do not chase
rumors or dress guesses as facts. Earth needs results. Execute the mission and
stay on comms.
